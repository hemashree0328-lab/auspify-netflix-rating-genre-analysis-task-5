# Auspify Task 5: Content Rating & Genre Analysis

## 1. Project Overview
This project explores Netflix content ratings and genres to understand the distribution of movies and TV shows across audience-suitability categories and genres.

## 2. Objectives
- Analyze content rating categories.
- Identify the most frequently represented genres.
- Compare rating distributions across movies and TV shows.
- Visualize genre and rating patterns.
- Generate data-driven observations.

## 3. Tools and Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib

## 4. Methodology
1. Load and clean the Netflix dataset.
2. Handle missing rating and genre information.
3. Count titles by rating category and content type.
4. Split genre lists into individual genres.
5. Compare genre distributions across movies and TV shows.
6. Generate charts and business insights.

## 5. Deliverables
- Rating analysis CSV
- Genre analysis CSV
- Genre-by-content-type CSV
- Rating comparison chart
- Top 10 genres chart
- Genre comparison chart
- Business insights summary

## 6. Conclusion
This project demonstrates how Python can be used to explore content classifications and genre distributions through data analysis and visualization.

## 7. Limitation
A title may belong to multiple genres. Content ratings describe audience suitability, not review scores or popularity. The findings reflect the dataset analyzed and do not establish actual viewing preferences.
